# VC Showcase Safety

Use these rules when a demonstration involves startups, founders, LPs, funds, investment research, deal flow, pitch decks, outreach, or investment decisions.

This is a prototype boundary, not legal, compliance, cybersecurity, or investment advice.

## Use safe demonstration data

- Use synthetic or clearly anonymized companies, founders, funds, messages, and financial figures by default.
- Do not upload a confidential pitch deck, cap table, data room, founder record, LP list, meeting note, private email, bank detail, signature, account credential, or unpublished fund information.
- Do not assume a public link is permitted to be copied into a third-party model. Check the source terms and the platform's data controls.
- Store a source name or URL and retrieval date for every external fact used in the result.
- Do not redistribute paid database content or private documents through the showcase.

If real confidential data enters the workflow, stop. State that these showcase rules are insufficient and request an approved data environment, access policy, retention rule, security review, and any required legal or compliance review.

## Keep facts separate from analysis

The output must distinguish:

- `Source facts`: information present in the supplied or retrieved material;
- `Missing information`: fields the source did not support;
- `AI interpretation`: comparisons, risks, questions, or hypotheses;
- `Human decision`: the reviewer's conclusion, recorded separately.

Do not convert missing data into a typical industry number. Do not present an estimate as company-reported. Show the source behind any score or classification.

## Do not automate consequential decisions

- AI may organize information, compare it with a stated thesis, identify gaps, and propose follow-up questions.
- AI must not silently make a final investment, rejection, allocation, valuation, suitability, or LP-admission decision.
- Avoid a single “objective” score. If scoring helps the demonstration, show the rubric, evidence for each component, uncertainty, and a human override.
- Do not infer race, ethnicity, religion, disability, health, sexual orientation, family status, age, or other protected or sensitive characteristics.
- Do not use a proxy such as name, photograph, location, school, accent, or career gap to infer those characteristics.
- Record corrections when a reviewer changes an extracted fact or model interpretation.

## Control communications and external actions

- Draft founder or LP outreach; do not send it automatically.
- Preview the recipient, subject, body, attachments, and data being disclosed before approval.
- Preview CRM changes, calendar invitations, document sharing, and status updates before execution.
- Use read-only integrations when they are sufficient.
- For a bulk action, show the full target count and require separate confirmation.
- Log the proposed action, approval, result, and error without logging private document contents or credentials.

## Label the demonstration

Use a visible note such as:

```text
Demonstration using synthetic data. AI output supports human review and may be
incomplete or incorrect. It is not an investment recommendation or a production
security assessment.
```

Before sharing, confirm that screenshots, URLs, exported files, logs, and sample prompts contain no real founder, LP, fund, or credential data.
